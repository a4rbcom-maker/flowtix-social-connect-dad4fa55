# Typing Dungeon: A Roguelike Powered by Your Keystrokes

**Summary:** Build a terminal-based roguelike where your real-world typing behavior—speed, accuracy, rhythm—directly controls combat, exploration, and loot.

- **Combat via keystroke:** Enemies attack in real time; your typing speed on prompted words determines damage dealt, while typos trigger counterattacks.
- **Biome by rhythm:** The dungeon's rooms shift based on your typing cadence—steady typers get calm libraries, frantic typers spawn chaotic fire zones.
- **Error economy:** Typos drop "glitch shards," a currency for buying risky power-ups that amplify mistakes' effects.
- **Replay ghost runs:** Record each run's keystroke stream and let players race their own ghosts.
- **Stretch goal:** Export a shareable "typing personality card" summarizing WPM, accuracy, and play style after death.
